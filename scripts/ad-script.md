# Coffee Advertisement Script
## 30-Second TV/Digital Ad

### SCENE 1: Morning Awakening (0-5 seconds)
**Visual**: Sunrise over misty coffee plantation  
**Voiceover**: "Every morning brings a new opportunity."
**SFX**: Gentle morning birds, soft music begins

---

### SCENE 2: The Brew (6-12 seconds)
**Visual**: Slow-motion pour of perfect dark coffee into white cup  
**Voiceover**: "Our premium beans, carefully roasted, deliver the perfect blend."
**SFX**: Coffee pouring sound, music intensifies

---

### SCENE 3: First Sip (13-18 seconds)
**Visual**: Close-up of satisfied customer enjoying their cup  
**Voiceover**: "Experience the richness. Feel the energy."
**SFX**: Satisfied sigh, uplifting music

---

### SCENE 4: Throughout the Day (19-27 seconds)
**Visual**: Quick montage: person working, smiling, accomplishing tasks  
**Voiceover**: "From sunrise to sunset, fuel your day with excellence."
**SFX**: Energetic, modern background track

---

### SCENE 5: Call-to-Action (28-30 seconds)
**Visual**: Product shot with logo/brand name  
**Voiceover**: "Choose Premium Coffee. Choose Excellence."
**SFX**: Brand jingle (2 sec)

---

## Notes
- Tone: Inspirational, energetic yet calm
- Target Audience: Coffee enthusiasts, professionals, morning routine people
- Color Palette: Warm browns, golds, white accents
