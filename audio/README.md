# Audio Assets

Store background music, voiceovers, and sound effects here.

## Background Music
- `background_music.mp3` - Main background track (30 sec)
- `brand_jingle.mp3` - Brand jingle (2 sec)

## Voiceover
- `voiceover.wav` - Full 30-second voiceover (professional recording)

## Sound Effects
- `coffee_pour.wav` - Coffee pouring sound
- `morning_birds.wav` - Morning ambience
- `positive_chime.wav` - Transition sound effect

## Instructions

1. Use high-quality audio files (44.1 kHz or higher)
2. Mix voiceover at -6dB below music
3. Ensure audio is properly balanced
4. Add sound effects for emphasis on key moments
5. Test audio levels before final render
